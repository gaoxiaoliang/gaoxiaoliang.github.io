From Beijing to Stockholm: A New Chapter in Sweden

March 7, 2026 · Xiaoliang Gao

I’ve always been fascinated by European culture, history, landscapes, cuisine, and so on. The idea of living in Europe for five to ten years—or even longer—to experience a different way of life has been a dream of mine for years.

That dream became reality in 2025, when my wife was admitted to a master’s program at KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. Together, we packed up our lives in Beijing and moved to Sweden.

Life in Stockholm#

Currently, I’m based in Stockholm, actively looking for software engineering opportunities. I’m open to positions in other cities across Sweden as well as remote work arrangements.

I’m excited about this new chapter and looking forward to contributing to a team where I can apply my experience in building distributed systems, enterprise applications, and AI-powered products.
